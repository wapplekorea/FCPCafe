### CamSwitcher

CamSwitcher syncs multicamera footage using shared audio and creates an editable first cut for Final Cut Pro. Camera A remains the timeline’s main audio, and optional screen recordings can be added as inserts when the picture changes. Video is processed locally on your Mac. Try it free for 14 days.
